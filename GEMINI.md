# Antigravity Agent Guidelines

## Content Preservation & Ground Truth Rule (Strict)
1. **Never Rewrite, Paraphrase, or Synthesize Factual Text**:
   - Biographical copy, degrees, academic credentials, institutional affiliations, titles, and monograph synopses are **immutable ground truth**.
   - Do not "polish", summarize, or rewrite author prose or headlines unless explicitly requested in words by the user.
2. **Zero Synthetic Resume Data**:
   - Never invent or infer degrees, past appointments, fellowships, or publications.
   - If an existing source file or downloaded page contains bio text, that text is strictly canonical and must be transferred verbatim.
3. **Redesign Scope Boundary**:
   - Visual redesigns (`/impeccable`, CSS, layout, typography) apply **only** to structure, markup, styling, responsiveness, and accessibility.
   - The body text, headings, citations, and links must remain 100% faithful to the original source.
